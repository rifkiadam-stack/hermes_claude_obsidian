# Islamic lectures: conditional guidance

Read with `transcript-sources.md` only when the source is an Islamic lecture or sermon. This guidance concerns source fidelity and attribution; it does not authorize issuing religious rulings or authenticating religious evidence.

## Drafting the note

- Distinguish the speaker's explanation, quoted Quranic verses/hadith, stories or analogies, differing views mentioned in the source, and agent reflection/synthesis.
- Preserve context and the speaker's qualifications. Do not turn advice into a universal legal ruling, an individual's explanation into consensus, or a summary into a fatwa.
- Use explicit attribution when needed: “the speaker explains…” rather than endorsing every explanation as verified fact.
- Do not guess Arabic text, transliteration, narrator names, verse numbers, hadith sources/grades, or schools of jurisprudence from unclear captions. Do not complete an utterance from model memory and present it as source content.
- If a quotation/reference is unclear but the idea is understandable, paraphrase with attribution and state that the reference is unverified. If the ambiguity affects the central meaning, flag it on the page and in the final report (never pause to ask); do not derive a legal conclusion from that passage.
- Clear Arabic text or reference numbers in a transcript may be recorded as quotations/references supplied by the source, but caption matching does not establish textual accuracy, translation accuracy, or hadith authenticity.
- Checking religious references against external sources is a separate task requiring approved scope and egress under the main skill. Separate external findings from what the speaker actually said and provide each with its own source/locator. Do not silently replace the raw quotation.
- Do not invent differences between schools or add theological discussions absent from the source merely to make the note appear complete.

## Verification and preview

- The coverage map remains mandatory. Explanations of verses/hadith or legal claims central to the note must trace to relevant raw-source passages.
- For ambiguous quotations/attribution or high-risk claims, prefer separate review when available. Another model's rereading is not religious authentication or independent evidence.
- Honestly state caption provenance, unclear passages, and reference-verification status. Avoid labels such as “verified religious evidence” when the only check was transcript matching.

## Placement and navigation

Follow active methodology settings and approved conventions in the selected vault. This portable reference does not prescribe a particular user, personal folder, or destination path.

Do not automatically create a lecture-specific category, folder, or MOC. Use only enabled, user-approved topic destinations and existing navigation from the setup registry. If destination rules are absent, unmapped or ambiguous, hold and report without asking; never choose an unregistered fallback or mutate vault configuration.
